About My Studies

I have always placed great emphasis on academic performance, which led me to National Taiwan University. My admiration for this university gradually faded when I began summer courses in June. The sweltering weather, competitive pressure, and confusion about my future direction...

However, it was also through this campus that I met a group of exceptional people who are highly capable yet humble, confident yet not arrogant. I asked myself when I truly recognized myself as a student of National Taiwan University—it wasn't the moment I received my student ID card, but rather when I genuinely accepted my imperfections and listened to the affirmation that every NTU student gave me during our interactions.

This allowed me to integrate into this campus. In the future, I will continue to delve deeper into my studies, work hard in my field of expertise, and strive to become one of the world's most outstanding talents.
